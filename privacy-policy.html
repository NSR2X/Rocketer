🚀 Rocketer Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us

Developer: Quantin BODIN

Overview

Rocketer is a Chrome extension that helps you track upcoming rocket launches. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data.

Key Point: Rocketer does not collect or store personal data. Any settings used by the extension remain local to your browser.

Data Collection

We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data about you.

Data Usage

We do not use your personal data for any purpose, because we do not collect it.

Data Storage

We do not store personal data. The extension may keep ephemeral, non-personal launch information in your browser memory to render the UI; nothing is sent to or kept on our servers.

Usage and Sharing

We do not share, sell, or transmit user data. We don’t handle your data—it's yours.

No personal data is collected or processed; therefore, nothing is shared.

External Services

Rocketer fetches public rocket launch information from:

  • The Space Devs API: Public launch schedules
  • RocketLaunch.live: Public launch schedules
  • Wikipedia: Backup launch information

These requests contain no personal information and are made anonymously.

User Control

Uninstalling the extension removes all local data related to it.

Updates

This privacy policy may be updated to reflect changes in our practices or Chrome Web Store requirements. Any changes will be posted here and in the extension's listing.
