## Detailed Description

**Never miss another rocket launch with Rocketer!**

Rocketer is the ultimate Chrome extension for space enthusiasts who want to stay up-to-date with all upcoming rocket launches from major space agencies and private companies.

### ✨ Key Features

🚀 **Real-time Launch Tracking**
- Comprehensive schedule of upcoming launches from SpaceX, NASA, ULA, Blue Origin, and more
- Live countdown timers showing exact time until liftoff
- Launch details including mission, location, and provider

🔔 **Smart Notifications**
- Customizable alerts before launch time (15 minutes to 2 hours)
- Browser notifications that won't let you miss important launches
- Filter notifications by your favorite space agencies

📺 **Live Stream Discovery**  
- Automatic detection of official live streams
- Picture-in-picture viewing for seamless multitasking
- Direct links to YouTube, Twitch, and official provider streams

⚙️ **Personalized Experience**
- Filter launches by provider (SpaceX, NASA, ULA, ESA, etc.)
- Compact layout option for more launches
- Sync settings across your Chrome browsers

🔒 **Privacy-Focused**
- All data stored locally on your device
- No tracking or analytics
- No personal information collected

### How It Works

1. **Install & Setup**: Add Rocketer to Chrome and customize your preferences
2. **Stay Informed**: Get notified about upcoming launches from your favorite providers
3. **Watch Live**: Click to open live streams in new tabs or picture-in-picture mode
4. **Never Miss**: Real-time countdown ensures you're always ready for liftoff

### Perfect For

- Space enthusiasts and rocket fans
- Students and educators in STEM fields
- Anyone interested in space exploration
- Content creators covering space topics

### Data Sources

Rocketer uses reliable public APIs and official sources:
- The Space Devs API for comprehensive launch data
- Wikipedia for backup launch information
- Official provider websites and social media
